## Join the NeuroBench community

NeuroBench is a community-driven benchmark project. Contributions are welcome across benchmarks, metrics, frameworks, and tooling.

- Mailing list: [neurobench@googlegroups.com](https://groups.google.com/g/neurobench)
- GitHub: [NeuroBench/neurobench](https://github.com/NeuroBench/neurobench)
- Docs: [Contributing guide](https://neurobench.readthedocs.io/en/latest/contributing.html)

## Ways to contribute

- Submit benchmark results and baselines.
- Add benchmark definitions, datasets, or evaluation scripts.
- Extend metric implementations and documentation.
- Improve framework integrations and examples.

## Suggested workflow

1. Review open issues and contribution docs.
2. Prototype your benchmark or metric extension in a branch.
3. Validate with harness examples and tests.
4. Open a pull request with benchmark details and expected outputs.
