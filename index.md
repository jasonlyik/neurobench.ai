  <section class="nb-section nb-animate">
    <h2>Why NeuroBench</h2>
    <div class="nb-card-grid nb-card-grid-3">
      <article class="nb-card">
        <h3>Collaborative by design</h3>
        <p>NeuroBench grows through community submissions across tasks, models, metrics, and frameworks.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="nb-card">
        <h3>Reproducible evaluation</h3>
        <p>The harness provides a consistent pipeline from data loading to metrics reporting across benchmarks.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="nb-card">
        <h3>Representative reporting</h3>
        <p>Benchmark outputs pair correctness with efficiency-oriented metrics to improve practical comparability.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="nb-section nb-animate">
    <h2>Two-track benchmark framework</h2>
    <div class="nb-track-grid">
      <article class="nb-track-card">
        <p class="nb-pill">Algorithm Track</p>
        <h3>Hardware-independent correctness and complexity</h3>
        <p>Evaluates benchmark tasks with consistent workload and static metrics, enabling cross-model comparison independent of deployment target.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="nb-track-card">
        <p class="nb-pill">System Track</p>
        <h3>Deployment-aware timing and efficiency</h3>
        <p>Captures real-time and systems-level performance. Benchmarks are defined and baseline submissions are being expanded.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </section>
